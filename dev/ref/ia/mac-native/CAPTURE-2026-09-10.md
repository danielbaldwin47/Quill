# Mac captures on the original rig — #343 and #344

Two questions the earlier runs left open, shot on the **built-in screen of the original 14-inch M1
MacBook Pro**, the display the committed captures were taken on.

- [**#344**](#344--the-window-narrows) asked which of the type and the measure gives when the
  window narrows, after [#328](CAPTURE-2026-09-09.md#328--matched-narrow-windows) saw the type
  shrink on a rig whose own positional control missed by 32 px. It reproduces, and the trigger is
  **the window's width alone**: three size classes, breaking at **440/441 pt** and **1250/1251 pt**,
  in the same places at two text sizes and at two line-length limits. The measure gives only
  afterwards, when the limit no longer fits.
- [**#343**](#343--the-templates-indent-and-em) asked whether iA's Classic indents a first line and
  what em its type is set at. **No Template indents one** — all four separate paragraphs by a gap —
  and Manuscript is the Editor's own grid to the pixel, while Classic's em depends on which of its
  metrics Source Serif 4 is asked to match, and the two answers are 11 % apart.

All numbers are **device pixels** at backing scale 2, relative to the capture named, unless labelled
pt. Bounds are `{left,top,right,bottom}`; regions are `[x,y,w,h]` in logical points.
[The manifest](capture-2026-09-10.json) names all 87 frames, their originals, both hashes, each
region and each colour check; `rig/measure_2026_09_10.py` verifies it and re-reads every number
below off the frames. The report records observations. It changes no Quill geometry, colour,
Template or Gate opponent.

*78 cells* is the requested container, `(limit + 14) × advance`; *gutter* and *measure* are in cells.

### The type gives, and it gives on the window's width alone

**1200 pt settles it.** At the default step a full container is 78 × 25.6 = 1997 px, which fits
inside a 1200 pt window's 2400 px with 400 px to spare — and the type shrinks anyway. Container
overflow cannot be the trigger; the window's own width is.

The break is exact and has no hysteresis: approached from 1512 and from 960, **1250 pt gives the
smaller type and 1251 pt the larger**, every time. A bisection from 240 pt (the narrowest window the
app allows) to 1512 pt finds **two** breaks, and finds them in the same two places at step 5, at
step 8 and with the limit at 80 — so neither the text size nor the measure moves them:

| Class | Window | Pitch at step 5 | Pitch at step 8 |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| narrowest | ≤ 440 pt | 53 | 76 |
| middle | 441 … 1250 pt | 63 | 92 |
| wide | ≥ 1251 pt | 73 | 109 |

Each class is a ladder of its own rather than a step of the wide one. The wide ladder
([NOTES § State 11](NOTES.md#state-11--every-text-size-the-app-offers)) has no 22.657 px cell
between step 4's 23.1 and step 3's 20.6, and no 35.2 between step 7's 35.7 and step 6's 30.7; and
`pitch / em` is tighter in the middle class than the wide ladder is at the same em — 1.668 against
1.711 at step 5, 1.568 against 1.607 at step 8. The scale from wide to middle is not constant
either: **0.885** at step 5 and **0.865** at step 8.

### The measure gives second, and only when the limit no longer fits

Above 440 pt the container is `min((limit + 14) cells, window − 20 px)` on all sixteen rows.

- Where the first term wins the gutter is **7.00 cells** and the measure is **the limit exactly** —
  64.0 or 80.1 cells — so the measure does not give at all: 960, 1040, 1200 and 1250 pt at step 5
  hold a full 64-character measure on a 22.657 px cell.
- Where the window wins, the container is the window less 10 px each side, the gutter falls to about
  **6 cells**, and the measure takes what is left: 42, 47, 56, 58 cells at step 8. That the gutter
  is 6 rather than 7 in most clipped rows but 7.00 at 1512 pt step 8 is measured and unexplained.

**440 pt is the one exception to the container rule.** Its container is 828 px where the window
would allow 860, and its gutter collapses to **one cell**, so a hanging `###### ` has nowhere to
hang. The narrowest class does something else with its margins; three frames do not say what.

### What this does not say

The two breaks are in window **points** on one display at backing scale 2.0. Whether they are points
or device pixels needs a second scale factor, which this rig has not got. The middle class's own
ladder is measured at two steps, not fourteen.

## #343 — the Templates' indent and em

**Dark, Preview → Web → Full, text size Default, window `{0,33,1512,982}`, region `[0,33,1512,949]`.**
Two passages, each shot in all four Manuscript-and-friends Templates, each with an Editor control
frame taken with Preview hidden:

- [`dev/ref/short.md`](../../short.md), whose **second paragraph follows a body paragraph** — the shape
  `dev/ref/sample.md` has not got, and the one an indented Template would indent.
- [`passage-template-em.md`](passage-template-em.md), whose paragraphs are runs of one glyph at
  three lengths. Two runs differing only in length cancel both side bearings, so the advance is the
  slope alone; and a run of `H` is a flat-topped block whose own ink height is the cap height.

| Template | H advance | n advance | n / H | H ink | H outline | Pitch | Paragraph step | First-line indent |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Modern (Sans) | 28.300 | 22.700 | 0.8021 | 28.46 | 29.75 | 69.0 | 136.0 | **none** (−1 px) |
| Classic (Serif) | 31.450 | 25.500 | 0.8108 | 28.51 | 29.80 | 69.3 | 134.5 | **none** (−1 px) |
| Manuscript (Duo) | 25.600 | 25.600 | 1.0000 | 28.49 | 29.78 | 73.3 | 145.5 | **none** (+3 px) |
| Manuscript (Mono) | 25.600 | 25.600 | 1.0000 | 28.49 | 29.78 | 73.3 | 145.5 | **none** (+2 px) |

*Indent* is the first line of a paragraph that follows a body paragraph, against the smallest left
edge of the lines beneath it; the few pixels either way are the first glyph's own side bearing.

### No Template indents a first line

**All four separate paragraphs by a gap and start every line on the same column.** Quill's Classic
is `indented` with no gap; iA's Classic is the other arrangement, and so are the other three. The
gap runs paragraph top to paragraph top at **1.94 pitches** in Classic (134.5 against 69.3), 1.97 in
Modern and **1.99** in Manuscript (145.5 against 73.3), read between three `H` runs, whose ink tops
are cap tops and so are directly comparable.

### The em, calibrated on the Editor

The Editor control carries the same runs at an em this repo already knows: its fills give a
**25.600 px** cell, which is 0.600 em on Mono's own tables, so **em = 42.667 px = 21.333 pt**. At
that em the outline of `H` stands 0.698 × 42.667 = 29.78 px, and its ink measures **28.49**. The ink
therefore sits **1.29 px inside the outline** on this ground and renderer, and the same correction
gives the outline column above.

**Manuscript is the Editor's own grid.** Its `H` run is pixel-identical to the Editor's — the same
249 / 505 / 761 px extents and the same per-row coverage — so Manuscript (Duo) and (Mono) both
render at **em 42.667 px = 21.333 pt** with a **73 px** pitch, which is `base × line_height` =
1.711. #261's Duo pitch of 72 px was one pixel out.

**All four Templates set the same cap height**, 28.46 … 28.51 px of ink across three different
faces, which is a 0.05 px spread. What differs between them is the advance.

**Classic's face is not Source Serif 4.** Its `n`/`H` advance ratio is **0.8108**, against IBM Plex
Serif's 0.8119 and Source Serif 4's 0.7690, and the app bundle ships `IBMPlexSerif-Regular.ttf`
beside a `style-IBMPlexSerif.css`. So there is no single em that puts Quill's Classic where iA's is:

| Source Serif 4 asked to match | Em | Line height against the 69.3 px pitch |
|---|---:|---:|
| iA Classic's **cap height** (29.80 px against SS4's 0.670) | **44.48 px = 22.24 pt** | 1.558 |
| iA Classic's **H advance** (31.450 px against SS4's 0.788) | **39.91 px = 19.96 pt** | 1.736 |

The two are **11 % apart**, and that gap is the Templates ticket's decision, not this run's.

### What this does not say

Preview is outside [ADR 0015](../../../../docs/adr/0015-the-design-oracle-outranks-the-parity-oracle.md)'s
reach: these are measured rendered states and they inform the Templates ticket rather than write a
`docs/design.md` row. The face identification rests on an advance ratio and a file name in the
bundle, not on the DOM. And the Plex advances are about 6.4 % narrower than the static
`IBMPlexSerif` and `IBMPlexSans` files give at the em their shared cap height implies — the same
factor for both faces and for both glyphs — so something in the Template's own CSS is compressing
them. Ink cannot say what.

## Repeating the run

`rig/README.md` has the permissions and the normalisation functions. Four things this run learned
that the rig did not already record:

- **The line-length limit is Settings → Editor, pop-up button 4**, and the Settings window's
  `entire contents` comes back empty on this SwiftUI pane — its controls answer only as
  `pop up button` of `scroll area 1 of window "Editor"`.
- **Preview's own control bar auto-hides**, so it cannot be asked what Preview is showing. The
  View menu's mark characters can, and `scroll area 1 of splitter group 1 of window 1` holding an
  `AXWebArea` is what says the Editor is gone.
- **A selection fill must sit on the row it was taken from.** Repeating `goto(downs=2, …)` without a
  reset walks two rows further down each time, and a fill that reaches a short row wraps and reads
  as one enormous box. `run_narrow.py` resets before every fill.
- **The window's own top edge reads as an ink row** the full width of the frame, and at 440 pt that
  width is under the 1800 px the earlier scripts filtered on. Filter it against the frame's width.

# The Lighthouse

The lamp had been lit for an hour before she noticed the boat. It was a *small thing*, a dark stitch on the water, and it was **not moving** the way boats move when someone is rowing them.

## What the sea keeps

> There are things the sea gives back and things it keeps, and no one has ever found the rule that decides between them.

- the rope, coiled and salt-stiff
- the good knife
- two oranges, for luck

1. Untie the skiff.
2. Push off before the tide turns.

She wrote the bearing on her sleeve, `NNE 22°`, and the log entry followed:

```
21:40  lamp lit
22:35  boat sighted, no oars
```

The rest is in [the keeper's book](https://example.org/keepers-book), and the boat that was not moving grew larger in the dark.

# The Spell check fixture dictionary

`hunspell/en_US.aff` and `hunspell/en_US.dic` are a small hunspell pair tagged `en_US`, holding every correctly spelled word of `ref/spell.md`, of the prose passage the bench regimes type (`PROSE` in `tools/regimes.mjs`) and of the document they type it into (`shots/latency/doc10k.md`), the corrections of the passage's eight misspellings so a suggestion has a word to offer, and none of the misspellings themselves; the `.aff` carries no affix rules, only the encoding, the suggestion alphabet and the word characters of the system's `en_US.aff`. Every test, shot and bench that touches a dictionary points `ENCHANT_CONFIG_DIR` at a temporary copy of this directory, so enchant finds this `en_US` before the machine's and an Add writes the copy, never the checkout. After editing either passage, regenerate the word list with `python3 ref/spell/regenerate.py`.
